# Moonwardens

The guild addon for the **Moonwardens**, a night elf RP-PvP guild on WoW Forever. It only works with other Moonwardens: messages from anyone outside the guild are ignored.

## Guild map
Guildies running the addon show as class-coloured dots on the world map and minimap, with a tooltip on hover. A side panel on the world map shows the layers the guild knows about in your zone.

## Layers (experimental)
The addon works out which layer you're on from the NPCs around you and shares it with the guild. Guildies on another layer show faded on the map. Layering on Forever isn't fully understood yet, so treat this as a best guess.

## Alerts
One button (or a keybind) calls nearby guildies for help. They get a banner, a sound and a pointer toward you. If they answer, they're invited to your group automatically, and the party becomes a raid when it fills up.

## Leaderboard
Horde killed in Ashenvale by you or your group, counted each week and shared across the guild. Honour doesn't matter: every kill counts. Resets every Tuesday.

## Stats
A snapshot of the guild: members online, races, classes and levels.

---

Type **/mw** for all commands. If the world map ever misbehaves, **/mw map off** turns the map features off.

Report bugs (and any red error text) to Krylea in the guild Discord.
